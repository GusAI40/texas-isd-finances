# Public usability protocol

Status: **UNVERIFIED**. This implementation authorizes no recruitment or
outreach. When ten consenting adult parents, taxpayers, or public readers are
available, include mobile users and people unfamiliar with school finance.

Ask participants, without coaching, to: find a district; interpret a
spending-per-student trend; compare like-for-like districts; distinguish a tax
rate from an illustrated tax bill; and find the source/year while identifying
modeled retention. Record only anonymous participant code, task, success, time,
assistance, device class, and comprehension error. Do not record names,
contacts, recordings, or free-form personal details. Targets are 8/10 unaided
success per task; median ≤60 seconds for the first four and ≤90 seconds for the
source/model task; no unresolved severe meaning inversion. Retest changed tasks
with fresh participants and preserve prior results.
