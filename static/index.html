    About this data — the honest version

    Where it comes from: the Texas Education Agency's "Summarized PEIMS Actual Financial Data" release, the state's official roll-up of every district's adopted financial reports, fiscal years 2009–2025.

    What the flags mean: math, not judgment. A "revenue drop" flag means revenue fell more than 15% from the prior year. Districts get flagged for good reasons all the time — a bond program ending, a one-time federal grant expiring, a boundary change.

    What this is NOT: an audit, an official TEA product, a rating system, or advice. Numbers can differ from a district's own books due to timing and reporting rules.

    Per-student math: we divide by fall enrollment. Districts with very small enrollment can show extreme per-student values — that's arithmetic, not scandal.
