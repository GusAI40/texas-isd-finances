    About this data — the honest version

    Source: TEA's "Summarized PEIMS Actual Financial Data" — the state's official roll-up of district financial reports, fiscal 2009–2025, downloaded from tea.texas.gov.

    Flags are math, not judgment. "Revenue drop" = revenue fell >15% vs the prior year. Districts get flagged for good reasons all the time — a bond ending, one-time federal funds expiring, a boundary change.

    Per-student math: totals ÷ fall enrollment. Small districts can show extreme per-student values — that's arithmetic, not scandal.

    Spending categories group TEA function codes: classroom instruction (functions 11, 95, 12, 13), leadership & administration (21, 23, 41, 92), student support (31–33), transportation (34), food (35), extracurricular (36), facilities (51), safety & technology (52, 53), community services (61).

    Not: an audit, an official TEA product, a rating, or advice.
