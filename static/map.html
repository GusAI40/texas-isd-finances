A seating chart for every school district in Texas

What am I looking at? Imagine every school district in Texas walked into one giant gymnasium and sat down next to the districts most like them. Big city districts sat together. Small country districts sat together. Fast-growing suburbs found each other.

That's this picture. Each dot is one district. Dots that sit near each other are alike — same size, same growth, same kind of funding. The bigger the dot, the more students. The darker the blue, the more it spends per student.

So what? When two dots sit right next to each other but one is pale and one is dark, two very similar districts are spending very differently. That's a question worth asking.
